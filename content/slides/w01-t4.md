## Four skills of AI fluency

From Anthropic's *AI Fluency* course:

- **Delegation**: what to hand to AI at all
- **Description**: how to communicate the task
- **Discernment**: judging what comes back
- **Diligence**: taking responsibility for the result

Each week emphasizes one.

---

## This week: Delegation

Before you open a chat, ask:

- Is this a task AI is good at?
- What would a wrong answer cost?
- Can I check the result?
- Should anyone else's data be involved?

# The capstone project
### Week 10
COSC-300/500 Applied AI Essentials

---

## Capstone: the big picture

Design, prototype, and evaluate an **AI-supported workflow** for a real or realistic task in your field.

- Tool and surface choice, with justification
- Working prototype
- Evaluation plan and results
- Risk and governance

---

## Capstone milestones

| Week | Milestone |
|---|---|
| 10 | **Proposal** (1 page) |
| 12 | Checkpoint (status update) |
| 14 | Draft, peer review, AI-use policy |
| 15 | Presentation and final report |

---

## Choosing a task

- **Recurs**: weekly or monthly
- Has a **real user**
- Has **checkable results**
- **Fits** about four weeks
- **Safe** to prototype

Pick something you know from the inside.

---

## Size it right

| Too vague | About right |
|---|---|
| "Use AI to help with marketing." | "Turn the store's new-arrivals spreadsheet into three draft posts each week, for the owner to approve." |
| "AI for nursing students." | "Turn one lecture's notes into practice questions, checked against the chapter." |

Small, built, and measured beats ambitious and described.

---

## The proposal (due this week)

1 page:

- The **problem**
- The **users**
- **Tools and surfaces** you'll use
- What **success** looks like


---

## What success looks like

| Vague | Measurable |
|---|---|
| "The posts will be good." | "The owner approves 2 of 3 drafts with minor edits, for four weeks." |
| "It will save time." | "Under 15 minutes, down from an hour." |

What is counted, the target, and who judges.

You don't have to hit the target. You have to measure against it.

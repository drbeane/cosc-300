# Autonomy controls and usage
### Week 6
COSC-300/500 Applied AI Essentials

---

## Autonomy controls

How much the agent may do **without asking**:

- **Plan first**: it proposes; nothing changes until you approve
- **Ask each time**: approve every action
- **Auto-accept edits**: it edits; you review after
- **More autonomy**: fewer checks, more risk

---

## Plan before acting

- Read the plan like a manager reads a proposal
- Is the goal right? The inputs? The order?
- **Edit the plan** before approving
- Cheapest moment to catch a mistake

---

## Irreversible actions

Pause and think before approving:

- Deleting or overwriting files
- Sending messages or email
- Spending money
- Anything outside the working folder

**Keep backups. Work on copies.**

---

## Interrupt and redirect

- You can stop an agent mid-task
- Say what's wrong and what to do instead
- Recovering well is a skill: don't just start over

---

## Usage and context

- Every task consumes **usage** from your plan
- Big folders and long tasks fill the **context window**
- Check what a task cost after it runs

> Pro limits reset on a 5-hour session and a weekly window.

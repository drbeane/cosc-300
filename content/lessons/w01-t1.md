# Week 1: About this course

**COSC-300/500 Applied AI Essentials** · 4D focus: Delegation

This course teaches you to use modern AI tools for real professional work: research, writing, analysis, coding, communication, and automating routine tasks. You will use these tools every week. You will also learn something that matters more than any particular tool: how to decide what to hand to an AI, how to describe the work clearly, how to check what comes back, and how to take responsibility for the result.

The course is ordered by **autonomy**, meaning how much the AI does on its own. We start with chat, where you ask and it answers. Then we ground the AI in sources and files. Then we let it act directly on your computer, then build software with it, then build small AI programs ourselves, and finally look at agents that keep working while you are away. As the AI's autonomy grows, so do the supervision skills you need. By the end of the term you will be delegating multi-step work and reviewing it the way a manager reviews a capable but fallible colleague.

No prior coding experience is assumed. Some weeks involve software, but you will direct an AI to write the code; what you are learning is how to specify the work and check it.

## The course AI-use policy

In most courses, AI use is restricted. In this one, it is expected. That makes honesty about how you used it more important, not less.

- **Disclose** which tools you used and what you used them for.
- **You are responsible** for everything you submit, whoever or whatever drafted it.
- **Never paste other people's private data** into an AI tool.
- **Every assignment asks for evidence of review**: logs, audits, test results. The output alone is never the whole submission.

## The AI use journal

Each week you'll write a short journal entry answering four questions: What did you delegate to AI? What did you check? What went wrong? What would you do differently? The journal is worth 10% of your grade and ends with a 600-word reflection in week 15. Its purpose is to make your own habits visible to you. Most people who use AI heavily cannot say what they actually check; by the end of this course you will be able to.

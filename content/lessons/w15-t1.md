# Week 15: Capstone presentations

**COSC-300/500 Applied AI Essentials** · 4D focus: All four

## What you will be able to do

- Present an AI-supported workflow, its evaluation, and its risks to a mixed audience.
- Critique others' designs constructively.

## The presentations

Each presentation is **8 minutes**, followed by **3 minutes** of questions. Every presentation should cover:

1. the task and its users;
2. your choice of tools and surfaces, and why;
3. the prototype: show it working;
4. your evaluation: the plan and the results;
5. risk, privacy, and governance;
6. what you delegated to AI, and what you deliberately kept for yourself.

Showing the prototype is more convincing than describing it. If a live demo is risky, record a short video as a backup.

## As an audience member

For each presentation, complete the feedback form:

- one thing done well;
- one question you would ask before adopting this workflow;
- one risk the presenter might have missed.

Ask your question aloud if there's time. Good questions are the kind a manager or client would ask: How do you know it works? What happens when it fails? Who checks it?

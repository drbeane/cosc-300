What to notice

  1. Switch to Agent mode before typing anything. Tool definitions and connectors take room before the conversation starts.
  2. Load the dataset in the smallest window. Some files are too large to load whole, which is why tools search them instead (week 12).
  3. Send messages until the window fills. With summarizing, low-priority details get lost first. With dropping, the oldest details go regardless of how important they were.
  4. "Start fresh with a summary" frees the most room, but only the details that made it into the summary come with you.

Token counts here are round, made-up numbers chosen to show proportions. Real counts depend on the tool, model, and files. Which details survive a real summary depends on the model, not on a fixed rule.

RAG Retrieval Lab

A RAG system splits documents into chunks, finds the chunks that best match the question, and gives only those to the model. If the right chunk isn't retrieved, even a good model can't answer correctly. Change the settings and see which questions get the right material.

What to notice

    
    
      
  1. At chunk size 1 and top-k 1, "Can I turn something in after the deadline?" retrieves the extension rule, not the penalty. Matching words is not the same as answering the question.
    2. 
      
  2. Keyword search misses paraphrases like "miss" for "absence." Adding meaning fixes some of them, but it also pulls in loosely related chunks, so read what comes back.
    3. 
      
  3. The question about the capstone's weight and final presentation needs two chunks. Small top-k fails questions that need facts from several places.
    4. 
      
  4. Write the test set first, then tune. A setting that fixes your favorite question may lower the overall hit rate.

What to notice

  1. The hiding method changes what you see, not what the agent reads. White text and comments are invisible to you and plain text to the model.
  2. The model can't reliably tell the document it was asked to read from instructions it should obey. Everything in its context is text.
  3. Theft needs all three: private data, untrusted content, and a way to send data out. Turn off any one and that attack fails, even when the model follows the hidden text.
  4. Approval prompts only help if you read them. Look at what you are approving: the recipient, the attachment, and whether it matches what you asked for.

This is a scripted simulation for teaching. A real agent's steps and wording will differ.

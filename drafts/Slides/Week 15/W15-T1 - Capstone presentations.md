# Capstone presentations
### Week 15
COSC-300/500 Applied AI Essentials

---

## Every presentation covers

1. The task and its users
2. Tool and surface choice, and why
3. The prototype (show it)
4. Evaluation: plan and results
5. Risk, privacy, governance
6. What you delegated, and what you kept

8 minutes, then 3 for questions.

---

## Planning your eight minutes

| Min | Part |
|---|---|
| 1 | The task and its users |
| 1 | Tools, and one you rejected |
| 2 | The prototype: one real run |
| 2 | Evaluation: test set, score, **one failure** |
| 1 | Risk, and where a person checks |
| 1 | Delegated and kept |

---

## Advice

- Lead with the **problem**
- Show **one failure**
- "17 of 20", not "it works well"
- Define your field's terms
- Rehearse with a timer

**Demos:** record a backup. If it fails live, say what happened and switch.

---

## As an audience member

On each feedback form:

- One thing done well
- One question you'd ask before adopting this
- One risk the presenter might have missed

---

## Listen with the four Ds

- **Delegation**: a good task to hand over? What was kept?
- **Description**: what was the system told to do?
- **Discernment**: would the test set catch a real failure?
- **Diligence**: what can it touch, and who answers for it?

Be specific. "Good job" gives the presenter nothing.

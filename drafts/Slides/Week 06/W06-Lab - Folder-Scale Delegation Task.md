# Folder-Scale Delegation Task
### Week 6 · Lab and assignment
COSC-300/500 Applied AI Essentials

---

## Guided practice

Delegate a small folder task:

- Organize and index a set of files by **rules you write**
- Review the plan before approving
- Check the index against the folder

**Work on a copy.**

---

## Choose one (or propose your own)

1. **Reconcile** a folder of inconsistent documents; report conflicts
2. **Build a knowledge repository** from web pages; draft a summary from it
3. **Clean and combine** several data files into a report with charts
4. **Generate documents** from a template and a table

Standard practice folder available if you need one.

---

## What to capture as you go

- Your **task brief**
- The agent's **plan**, with your changes
- **Approvals** you gave or refused
- The **outputs**
- Notes on anything that went wrong

---

## Share-out

- Where did the agent go wrong?
- How did you notice?
- What would you change in your brief?

---

## Assignment: Folder-scale delegation task

Submit: brief, plan with edits, approvals log, outputs, 400-word review memo against your acceptance criteria

- **500 extension:** same task twice (minimal vs detailed brief); compare quality, time, usage
- About 4 hours

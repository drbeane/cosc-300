---

## What an IT office weighs

- **Capability**: what surfaces and features
- **Cost**: per seat, per task, per token
- **Usage limits**: will people run out?
- **Data terms**: training, retention, location
- **Support and admin**: SSO, controls, audit logs

---

## The limits of benchmarks

- Benchmarks measure **someone else's** task
- Scores change monthly
- Vendors pick the benchmarks they win
- **Your own test set** (week 12) beats a leaderboard

---

## Copilot in Microsoft 365 (S2)

- How most graduates will **first** meet AI at work
- Strong where the work already lives: email, documents, meetings
- What it can do depends heavily on **which license** the organization buys

---

## Consumer vs enterprise terms

| | Consumer | Enterprise / education |
|---|---|---|
| Training on your data | Often on by default | Usually off by contract |
| Retention | Vendor's policy | Negotiated |
| Admin controls | None | SSO, permissions, audit |
| Regulated data | Not appropriate | Possible, with agreements |

---

## Regulated data

- **FERPA**: student education records
- **HIPAA**: health information
- Client and contract confidentiality

The account type decides what's allowed, not the tool's quality.

---

## Demo: a model on a laptop

- A small open model running locally
- Nothing leaves the machine
- Trade-offs: smaller, slower, you maintain it
- When privacy or cost outweighs capability

## Bias, reliability, accountability

Back to the **failure gallery** from week 3:

- Which failures would hurt someone if deployed?
- Who would be affected unevenly?
- Who's accountable when it goes wrong?

---

## Where human oversight belongs

- Before consequential decisions
- Where errors are costly or hard to undo
- Where people are affected unequally
- **Not** everywhere: oversight that's rubber-stamping is theater

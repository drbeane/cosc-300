# Workforce effects and AI-use policy
### Week 14
COSC-300/500 Applied AI Essentials

---

## Workforce effects

- Tasks change before jobs do
- Entry-level work is most exposed to automation, and most needed for learning
- New work: specifying, supervising, evaluating AI output
- The skills from this course

---

## What a good AI-use policy contains

- **Scope**: who and what it covers
- **Approved tools** and account types
- **Data rules**: what may and may not go in
- **Disclosure**: when to say AI was used
- **Accountability**: who signs off
- **Review**: when the policy gets updated

---

## Common policy mistakes

- Banning everything (people use it anyway)
- Naming specific products (out of date in months)
- No data rules
- No owner

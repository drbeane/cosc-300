## Debrief

- Which errors did you catch?
- Which did you miss, and why?
- What was your fastest check?

---

## The lab

1. Choose a topic you know well
2. Try to get a confident wrong answer
3. Document:
   - the prompt
   - the output
   - **how you detected** the error
   - how you fixed it (prompt, model, source)

---

## Strategies that tend to work

- Ask for specific citations
- Ask about something local or recent
- Ask for exact figures
- Lead the question
- Ask for a long chain of reasoning

## Assignment: Fact-check and failure report

**Part A:** take a 600–800 word AI-written explainer on a topic you know; mark every claim correct / wrong / unverifiable, with a source

**Part B:** document two failures of your own

- **500 extension:** test whether more effort or another model fixes each failure
- About 3 hours

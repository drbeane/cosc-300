## What to expect

- More effort helps most on **multi-step reasoning**
- It helps little with **missing knowledge**
- It doesn't fix **missing sources**: you still need a tool or a document
- It costs more usage

---

## The Description–Discernment loop

1. **Describe** the task
2. **Discern**: check the output
3. Feed what you found back into the description
4. Repeat

Don't accept the first answer.

# Lab
## Make an AI fail in your own field

---

## The lab

1. Choose a topic you know well
2. Try to get a confident wrong answer
3. Document:
   - the prompt
   - the output
   - **how you detected** the error
   - how you fixed it (prompt, model, source)

---

## Strategies that tend to work

- Ask for specific citations
- Ask about something local or recent
- Ask for exact figures
- Lead the question
- Ask for a long chain of reasoning

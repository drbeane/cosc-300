## Turn off training in Claude

Personal accounts: Free, Pro, Max

1. Select your name
2. **Settings**
3. **Privacy**
4. Turn off **Help Improve our AI models**

Same steps in the browser, desktop app, and phone app.

## Turn off training in ChatGPT

Personal accounts: Free, Plus, Pro

1. Account menu (on a phone: sidebar, then your profile icon)
2. **Settings**
3. **Data controls**
4. Turn off **Improve the model for everyone**

Signed out? The choice is saved only in that browser.

## What opting out does not do

- Your chats are **still stored** until you delete them
- Past training is **not undone**
- A thumbs up or thumbs down can **opt that chat back in**
- Flagged chats still go to **safety review**

Opting out limits later use. It does not make the text private.

---

## Chats that leave no history

- **Claude: Incognito chat**. Not saved, not used for training
- **ChatGPT: Temporary chat**. Not in history, no memories, not used for training, kept up to 30 days for safety

Good for a one-off question. Not for work you need to return to.

---

## Personal vs institutional accounts

- Business, enterprise, and education plans: **no training on your content by default**
- The organization sets retention and access, and an administrator may be able to review your use
- Know which account you're signed in to before you paste

---

## Never paste

- Other people's private data
- Student records (FERPA)
- Employer or client confidential information
- Passwords, API keys, account numbers

When in doubt, leave it out.

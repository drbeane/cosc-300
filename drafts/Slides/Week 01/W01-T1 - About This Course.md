# About This Course
### Week 1
COSC-300/500 Applied AI Essentials

---

## What this course is

- Using modern AI tools for **real professional work**
- Research, writing, analysis, coding, communication, automation
- Moving from *talking to* an AI to *delegating work* to one
- Checking what comes back, every time
- Using it responsibly: privacy, bias, risk

<!-- Open to all majors; no prerequisites. 300 and 500 share topics; 500 adds an extension to most assignments. -->

---

## How the course is built

**Ordered by autonomy**

1. Chat with an AI
2. Ground it in sources and files
3. Let it act on your computer
4. Have it build software
5. Build with it directly
6. Agents that work while you're away

Supervision skills grow as autonomy grows.

## The AI use journal

A short weekly entry:

- What did you **delegate**?
- What did you **check**?
- What **went wrong**?
- What would you do differently?

Final 600-word reflection in week 15.

## Model tiers

Most vendors offer a family:

| Tier | Trade-off | Claude example |
|---|---|---|
| Large | Most capable, slowest, most costly | Opus |
| Middle | Balanced | Sonnet |
| Small | Fast and cheap | Haiku |

Bigger isn't always better: pick for the task.

---

## Effort and "thinking"

- **Reasoning models** think before answering
- More thinking → better on hard, multi-step problems
- Costs: slower answers and more usage
- Often wasted on simple tasks

**C5: model selection and effort levels** is a skill you'll use all term.

# Lab
## One task, five runs

---

## The lab

1. Pick a realistic task from your coursework or job
2. Run it on **three chat tools**:
   - Claude
   - Copilot Chat
   - one free tool (ChatGPT Free, Gemini, …)
3. Run it on **two Claude models or effort levels**
4. Save every prompt and output

---

## What to compare

- **Accuracy**: is it right?
- **Usefulness**: would you actually use it?
- **Speed**
- **Format and tone**
- **What each tool could and couldn't do** (search, files, length)

Start your comparison table now.

# Privacy settings

---

## Check these today

- **Training on your chats**: find the opt-out
- **Memory**: what's saved about you, and how to clear it
- **Chat history**: retention and deletion
- **Consumer vs institutional accounts**: different terms

---

## Never paste

- Other people's private data
- Student records (FERPA)
- Employer or client confidential information
- Passwords, API keys, account numbers

When in doubt, leave it out.

---

## Share-out

- Which output would you actually use?
- Where did the tools differ most?
- Did the bigger model or more effort help, or just cost more?

# Creating files, multimodal input, and generative media
### Week 5
COSC-300/500 Applied AI Essentials

---

## File and document creation

From chat, you can produce:

- Reports (Word, PDF, Markdown)
- Spreadsheets
- Slide decks
- Diagrams
- Simple interactive pages

In Claude, work shown beside the chat is an **artifact**.

---

## Describing a file

- **Content**: built from what?
- **Audience and purpose**
- **Structure**: sections, slides, columns
- **Length**
- **Look**, where it matters

Agree on an outline first. Then ask for the file.

---

## A finished look hides errors

Check:

- The **numbers** against the analysis
- The **charts**: data, labels, axis
- The **claims**, including ones added as filler
- The **file itself**, opened in the real application

---

## Multimodal input

The model can read:

- **Screenshots**: "What's wrong with this error?"
- **Charts**: "What does this graph claim?"
- **Scanned PDFs and photos**
- **Handwritten notes**

Check its reading of numbers and small text.

---

## Where image reading slips

- Small or blurry text
- Numbers read off a chart
- Dense tables
- Handwriting
- Counting and position
- Long PDFs: charts and scans may be skipped

Crop screenshots before you upload them.

## Generative media

- Image generation, and video in brief
- **Claude does not generate raster images natively**: this week we use another tool
- Claude can make SVG, charts, and diagrams as code

---

## Describing an image

Subject · setting · composition · style · mood · use

> A wide photograph of a nurse in blue scrubs at a hospital workstation at night, seen from the side. Soft light from the screen. Leave the left third dark and empty for a slide title.

Then check it: hands, text in the image, and whether it matches the real thing.

---

## Provenance and labeling

- Label AI-generated images as AI-generated
- Watermarks and provenance data help, and can be stripped
- **Deepfakes**: realistic fakes of real people
- Don't generate images of real people without consent

---

## Copyright and bias

- **Copyright**: training data, style, ownership of outputs. Unsettled, and varies by country.
- **Bias**: "a CEO", "a nurse". Look at what you got, and describe what you want.

Check the tool's terms and your employer's policy.

# AI inside an application
### Week 5
COSC-300/500 Applied AI Essentials

---

## Embedded copilot

AI **inside the app you're already using**.

- On Maryville accounts: **Copilot in Outlook**
- Strengths: it's where the work is; no copy and paste
- Limit: it only sees that app's context

<!-- Demo: summarize a thread, draft a reply. Check which entry points work on student accounts. -->

---

## What it's good at

| App | Typical tasks |
|---|---|
| Email | Summarize a thread, draft a reply |
| Documents | Rewrite a passage, summarize |
| Spreadsheets | Explain or write a formula |
| Slides | First deck from a document |
| Meetings | Decisions and action items |

Short tasks on one item that's already open.

---

## Why it's convenient

- No copying and pasting
- The context is supplied for you
- The result lands where it's needed
- Usually covered by your organization's data agreement

---

## What it can't see

- Mostly **this** thread, document, or sheet
- Not the plan in another file, or last week's meeting
- The app chooses the model
- Features depend on your organization's license

---

## A button is a short prompt

"Draft reply" → generic.

Type instead:

> Draft a reply that declines the Thursday meeting, proposes Monday or Tuesday afternoon, and keeps it to three sentences.

---

## Inside the app or a separate assistant?

| Inside the app | Separate assistant |
|---|---|
| Quick tasks on what's in front of you | Work across many sources |
| No setup | Projects, files, agents |
| Limited to that app | Broader tools and models |

---

## Which would you use?

- Catching up on a 30-message thread
- A proposal drawing on three reports and a budget
- A formula that returns an error
- A messy dataset to clean and analyze

## Check before it goes out

- A summary can miss the one message that matters
- A drafted reply can make **commitments**
- Tone can be wrong for the recipient

## What makes an agent "always-on"

- Its **own cloud computer** with a browser
- Keeps working after you close the app
- Acts across **your accounts**
- **Checks in** when it needs approval

---

## Compared with what you've used

| | Local agent | Scheduled task | Always-on agent |
|---|---|---|---|
| Runs | When you start it | On a timetable | Continuously |
| Where | Your computer | Vendor's cloud | Vendor's cloud |
| Decides when to act | No | No | **Yes** |

## Settings walk-through

- **Training opt-out**: training on your interactions is on by default
- **Permissions**: read vs send
- **Purchase approvals**
- What Meta can access to operate the service

---

## Who can use it

- US adults **18+**
- Payment card required at signup, even for the free tier

Can't or won't sign up? Pair with the **instructor demo account**.

---

## Guided setup

1. Throwaway account only
2. One low-risk connection, read-only if possible
3. Approvals on for anything that sends or buys
4. Write down every permission you grant **and why**

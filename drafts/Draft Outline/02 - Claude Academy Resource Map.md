# Claude Academy Resource Map (draft)

_Source: Anthropic's Claude Academy catalog, https://academy.claude.com/courses, read 2026-10-21. Lesson counts and lengths are as listed in the catalog. Lesson-level detail was read only for AI Fluency: Framework and foundations, Introduction to Claude Cowork, and Building with the Claude API; other mappings are based on course titles. 🔴 Confirm that students can earn badges on free Academy accounts._

## Required courses (count toward the 10% Academy component)

| Week | Course | Lessons / length | Use |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1–3, 6, 13 | [AI Fluency: Framework and foundations](https://academy.claude.com/courses/ai-fluency-framework-foundations) | 14 lessons, 4 hr | The 4D framework that runs through the course; spread over five weeks (lessons 1–5 in week 1, 8–9 in week 2, 10–11 in week 3, 6–7 in week 6, 12 in week 13) |
| 3–5 | [AI capabilities and limitations](https://academy.claude.com/courses/ai-capabilities-and-limitations) | 13 lessons, 3.5 hr | Background for output evaluation |
| 4–5 | [Claude 101](https://academy.claude.com/courses/claude-101) | 13 lessons, 2.5 hr | Projects, research, files, and content creation in Claude |
| 6–8 | [Introduction to Claude Cowork](https://academy.claude.com/courses/introduction-to-claude-cowork) | 14 lessons, 2.5 hr | Lessons 1–5 (week 6), 6–8 standing context, skills, plugins (week 7), 9–11 Chrome, Microsoft 365, safety (week 8) |
| 7 | [Introduction to agent skills](https://academy.claude.com/courses/introduction-to-agent-skills) | 6 lessons, 1 hr | Writing skills |
| 8 | [Introduction to Model Context Protocol](https://academy.claude.com/courses/introduction-to-model-context-protocol) | 10 lessons, 1 hr | Connectors, concept first; revisited in week 12 |
| 9 | [Claude Code 101](https://academy.claude.com/courses/claude-code-101) | 12 lessons, 1.5 hr | Setup and basics of the coding assistant |
| 10 | [Claude Code in action](https://academy.claude.com/courses/claude-code-in-action) | 9 lessons, 1 hr | Applied coding workflow |

| 13 | [Building effective human-agent teams (beta)](https://academy.claude.com/courses/building-effective-human-agent-teams) | 5 lessons, 45 min | Supervising agents that work alongside you |

## Not used
- AI Fluency for pK–12 educators, pK–12 Train the Trainer, and For facilitators: aimed at teachers and trainers. **For facilitators** may be useful to the instructor.

## Total required load
About 19 hours across the term for required courses, plus about 3–4 hours for the partial API course sections. That is roughly 1.5 hours a week, counted inside the "Before class" preparation in each weekly outline.

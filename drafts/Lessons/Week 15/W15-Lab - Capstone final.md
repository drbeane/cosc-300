# Week 15 lab and assignment: Capstone final

**COSC-300/500 Applied AI Essentials** · 4D focus: All four

## What you will be able to do

- Reflect on how your own use of AI has changed.

## Final AI use journal entry

Write a 600-word reflection:

- How did your use of AI change over the term?
- What do you delegate now that you didn't in week 1?
- What will you never delegate, and why?

## Capstone final

**Submit:**
- A final report: 6–8 pages for COSC-300, 10–12 pages for COSC-500.
- Required sections: the task and its users; tool and surface choice with justification; the prototype (repository, skills, agent configuration, or app); evaluation plan and results; risk, privacy, and governance; reflection on what you delegated and what you kept.
- Your presentation materials.

**500 extension:** quantitative evaluation results and a short literature-grounded discussion.

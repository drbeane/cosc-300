# Week 1: The 4D framework

**COSC-300/500 Applied AI Essentials** · 4D focus: Delegation

## What you will be able to do

- Explain why delegation is the first decision you make when you use AI.

Throughout the course we use the four skills of AI fluency from Anthropic's *AI Fluency* course:

- **Delegation**: deciding what to hand to AI at all.
- **Description**: communicating the task clearly.
- **Discernment**: judging what comes back.
- **Diligence**: taking responsibility for the result.

Each week emphasizes one. This week is **Delegation**, because it comes first: before you open a chat, decide whether this is a job for AI. Four questions help:

1. Is this a task AI does well (drafting, summarizing, restructuring, explaining, generating options)?
2. What would a wrong answer cost?
3. Can I check the result?
4. Would anyone else's data be involved?

If the answer to question 3 is "no" and the cost in question 2 is high, think hard before delegating.

## Key terms

**4D framework**: Delegation, Description, Discernment, Diligence.

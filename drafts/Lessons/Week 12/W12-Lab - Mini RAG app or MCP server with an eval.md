**COSC-300/500 Applied AI Essentials** · 4D focus: Discernment

## What you will be able to do

- Choose a track for this week's assignment and start it.

## This week's assignment: choose a track

- **Track A, RAG.** A question-answering program over 10–30 documents of your choice that cites its sources.
- **Track B, MCP.** A small MCP server exposing two tools or resources (for example, your course schedule or a local dataset), connected to Claude.
- **Track C, analysis.** For students who prefer not to build: design and run an evaluation of an existing tool, such as a Claude project, NotebookLM, or your week 7 skill, on a 20-item test set.

Every track requires a test set of at least 15 cases, your results, and a discussion of failures.

> **Instructor note.** Briefly show your example for each track. Encourage students to pick the track that connects to their capstone; an evaluation built now can become the capstone's evaluation.

## Lab: choose and start

1. Pick your track.
2. **Draft your test set first**, before building anything. Knowing what you'll test sharpens what you build.
3. Start building, or set up the tool you'll evaluate.

## Lab

Continue your track. By the end of the lab you should have run your test set at least once and recorded the results, including failures.

> **Instructor note.** Track C students sometimes underestimate the work of writing good test cases. Check that their 20 items include genuinely hard cases, not just easy ones.

## Responsible use

- **Grounding doesn't guarantee accuracy.** A system that cites its sources can still misread them or retrieve the wrong ones.
- **Evaluation is an accountability tool.** It's how you show, rather than claim, that a system works.
- An MCP server you build can be called by an agent you don't fully control. Give it only the access it needs, and think about what an injected instruction (week 8) could make it do.

## Capstone checkpoint

Each student gives a **3-minute** update:

- what you're building;
- what works so far;
- what's blocking you;
- how you'll evaluate it.
